About the lab

We connect cognitive science, neuroscience, and AI to explain intelligent behavior.

Based at the School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences, Peking University, our group brings together psychology, cognitive neuroscience, computational neuroscience, data science, artificial intelligence, and social-behavioral research.

We combine behavioral experiments, neural measurements, computational modeling, and AI systems to study perception, reasoning, value decision-making, and interactive intelligence. Our goal is to develop explanatory models of the human mind and translate effective brain computations into interpretable, human-compatible algorithms.

Research directions

Three linked programs, one question: how intelligence becomes useful action.

01

Visual Cognition and Reasoning

We investigate how people interpret objects, agents, scenes, emotions, physical events, and social signals, and how these representations support judgment, learning, and action.

02

Neural Decoding and Interpretable Brain Algorithms

We track information processing in the human brain with high temporal precision, link neural dynamics to behavior, and extract algorithmic principles that remain interpretable.

03

Human-like Autonomous AI Agents

We build value-guided agents that connect perception, evidence seeking, social meaning, and goal planning for more natural, personalized, and long-horizon interaction.

Principle 01

Start with human behavior, then ask what computations could produce it.

Principle 02

Use neural data to constrain models, not merely decorate them.

Principle 03

Translate mechanisms into AI systems that remain interpretable and human-compatible.

Technical approach

From human data to models that can be tested, interpreted, and deployed.

01

Behavioral experiments and computational task design for complex cognition.

02

MEG, neuroimaging, and model-based neural decoding at fine temporal scales.

03

Brain-AI alignment, representation discovery, and intervention in neural networks.

04

Closed-loop systems for brain-computer interfaces, intelligent feedback, and human-AI collaboration.
